\chapter{Literature Review}\label{ch:literature-review}


\section{Agent Benchmarking Frameworks}\label{sec:agent-benchmarking-frameworks}
- **Static Benchmarks:** Limitations of HELM and MMLU.
- **Agentic Frameworks:** Analysis of AgentBench, ToolBench, and GAIA.
- **Trajectory Analysis:** The shift toward process-based evaluation (OpenTelemetry standards).
\section{LLM-as-a-Judge \& Reliability}\label{sec:llm-as-a-judge-&-reliability}
- **The Judge Paradigm:** Using strong models (GPT-4o) to evaluate weaker models.
- **Bias Mitigation:** Addressing Positional Bias, Verbosity Bias, and Self-Preference.
- **Calibration:** Techniques for aligning synthetic judges with limited human gold labels.
\section{Ranking Algorithms}\label{sec:ranking-algorithms}
- **Zero-Sum Foundations:** ELO and its logistical assumptions.
- **Bayesian Approaches:** TrueSkill and the **Bradley-Terry Model** for handling uncertainty in sparse data.
- **Multidimensional Ranking:** Vectorized approaches (Blade-Chest, mELO) for intransitive interactions.
\section{Taxonomy \& Routing}\label{sec:taxonomy-&-routing}
- **Intent Classification:** Unsupervised clustering and embedding-based routing for domain inference.
